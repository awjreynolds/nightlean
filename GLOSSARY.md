# Glossary

- Compact: Midnight's smart contract language. Its compiler generates zero-knowledge circuits used to prove contract interactions with the ledger. [Compact overview](https://docs.midnight.network/compact)
- ZKIR: Midnight's zero-knowledge intermediate representation for proofs. ZKIR version 3 is the current backend for ledger 9. It is separate from the on-chain runtime. [Ledger 9 upgrade notes](https://docs.midnight.network/relnotes/ledger-9)
- Impact VM: Midnight's on-chain execution environment, also called the on-chain runtime. Its version is tracked separately from ZKIR. [Ledger 9 upgrade notes](https://docs.midnight.network/relnotes/ledger-9)
- Ledger era: A versioned Midnight network compatibility target, such as ledger 9, which determines compatible Compact toolchain, runtime, proof, and client versions. [Compatibility matrix](https://docs.midnight.network/relnotes/support-matrix)
